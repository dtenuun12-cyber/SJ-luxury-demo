Our Craftsmanship

From Timber to Heirloom

Selecting the Timber

Rosewood is chosen and aged for years before it meets a chisel — grain harmony, density, and stability precede form.

Traditional Joinery

Mortise-and-tenon construction throughout — zero metal fasteners, allowing wood to expand naturally across centuries.

Hand Carving

Motifs are added sparingly and precisely in the Ming tradition — each element sculpted entirely by hand.

Private Delivery

White-glove placement inside your residence, accompanied by an artisan care guide for long-term preservation.
