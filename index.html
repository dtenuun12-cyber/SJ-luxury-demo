Our Craft

From Timber to Heirloom

Selecting the Timber

Rosewood is chosen and aged for years before it ever meets a chisel — grain, density, and stability matter more than appearance alone.

Traditional Joinery

Mortise-and-tenon construction throughout — no nails, no metal fasteners, allowing the wood to move naturally across decades.

Hand Carving

Ornament is added sparingly and precisely, in the Ming aesthetic tradition — each motif carved entirely by hand, never machined.

Private Delivery

White-glove placement inside your home, alongside guidance on care so the piece is properly settled into its new life.
